# Botanical architectural surround

## Supplied files

- **levitate-frame-original.png** — original generated image, 1536 × 1024 pixels.
- **levitate-frame.webp** — the same artwork encoded for the web, 105,998 bytes. Encoded at quality 83 with metadata removed; no creative retouching or crop was applied.

The original was generated with the built-in image generation tool during the Field Notes identity work. Keep the original as the visual source. V3 reuses this selected artwork rather than generating a substitute.

## Generation brief

A straight-on architectural aperture in dark pine-green stone and blackened green metal. Substantial material planes, precise asymmetric bevels and deep shadow cuts. Large restrained botanical leaf-vein relief at the upper left and lower right. Warm amber light grazes the inner left and lower edges. One small lacquer-red inlay interrupts the lower-right material.

The central rectangle is quiet and will be covered by an opaque, live paper interface. All meaningful detail belongs at the periphery. Corners contain the richest detail; middle edges stay continuous enough to stretch. The mood is intimidating competence expressed through warmth, weight and exactness.

The image contains no logo, text or controls. It is artwork for a frame; it is not a flattened website screenshot.

## Use

Use 15% vertical and 10% horizontal border-image slices, stretched with equal visual border widths. At a 320px component use about 20px borders; at 1024px use 78px. The center remains ordinary opaque paper. Leave the image's center unpainted by omitting the CSS border-image `fill` keyword.

Use the full treatment at major entry points. Reading and documentation pages return to paper and precise editorial structure. Amber is a material-light effect inside the artwork, not an additional interface accent color. Acorn does not automatically inherit the Levitate-specific leaf relief.
